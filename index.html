The Space

Casa Luz — 45 Howard Street, 2nd Floor, SoHo.

Why it works for this group

Casa Luz's 2nd floor is a 3,500 sq. ft. open loft with exposed brick and exceptional natural light — rare for SoHo. The format is fully flexible: open it for movement and somatics in the morning, close it into a focused boardroom configuration for afternoon strategy. The space holds 14 comfortably across both modes.

A prep kitchen with industrial sink, refrigerator, and stainless steel prep tables supports drop-off catering with on-site plating and setup. The building has an elevator for easy load-in.
